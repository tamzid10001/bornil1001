company

we are a small team in dhaka. we build a foundation model that reads, writes and reasons in bangla first, and this assistant on top of it.

hundreds of millions know bangla first-hand. most large models learnt it second-hand. they speak it, and it shows.

ours learns from writing that began in bangla, and the people who wrote it come first. chatgaiya, sylheti and barishailla hang at the same height as standard bangla.

a source is our reph, a small mark above the line. a claim without one is misspelt. nothing unmeasured reaches the page — on the screens above, one rule under a figure means it is still being checked, and two mean it was checked against its source.

the line has room for a few more researchers, engineers and linguists who want to do their life's work in bangla. write to shayan.ar@bornil.io, or read the longer note.
