bangla letters hang from one line, the matra. a word is letters joined along it.

বর্ণিল is built the same way. everything hangs from one line of work: a foundation model that reads, writes and reasons in bangla first, and an assistant on top of it. there is no second line.

hundreds of millions know bangla first-hand. most large models learnt it second-hand. they speak it, and it shows.

ours learns from writing that began in bangla, and the people who wrote it come first. chatgaiya, sylheti and barishailla hang at the same height as standard bangla.

a source is our reph, a small mark above the line. a claim without one is misspelt. one rule under a figure means it is still being checked. two mean it was checked against its source. nothing unmeasured reaches the page.

বর্ণিল means colourful. its root means a letter, and also a colour. we keep them in that order. a source takes its colour once it has been read. graphite is our hand. blue ink is yours, where you act.

we are a small team in dhaka. our attention is long. the line has room for a few more researchers, engineers and linguists who want to do their life's work in bangla. write to shayan.ra@bornil.io.

constants: 11 vowels and 39 consonants, as taught in bangladesh. one matra. the rest we measure.
